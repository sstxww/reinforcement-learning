# V5 proposal: distinguish missing BUY actions from missing BUY opportunities

Source: prior independent PR #60 (low-learning-rate candidate; three complete held-out games; 0 purchases). That evidence does not reveal whether a buy was mask-legal at each decision.

Implement an isolated real-game audit on a published unpromoted PPO checkpoint and the main BaselinePolicy. Track BUY legal exposure using the pinned 8..15 band, actions actually chosen, policy END_TURN while BUY remained legal, guard END_TURN, and per-turn aggregates. Two newly held-out seeds are a diagnostic smoke test, **not** a strength demonstration.

Possible interpretations (not proven):
- Many legal BUY exposures + frequent policy END_TURN with BUY legal: candidate policy prematurely exits despite a purchase opportunity.
- Few BUY-legal exposures: possible game economy/board progression or mask issue; a zero BUY count alone is not model pathology.
- Many forced ends: candidate loops; full-game guarded finishes are not pure-policy wins.

Do not promote the model or train more PPO without real evidence and comparable baselines. Real evidence must be supplied by the workflow artifact, not synthetic unit tests.

Model ranks were [8,8], baseline [6,7]. Raw actions 1 and 2 accounted for 222/248 model decisions. All four games completed without model safety overrides. Original source is [finite artifact](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41428098018/artifacts/13132408776); compressed essential evidence is committed to `reports/s-20261102-83d91476/001-buy-opportunity-report.json`.

This **rules out lack of legal BUY opportunities in these games**, not other root causes.
