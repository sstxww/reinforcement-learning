Raw JSON: reports/s-20261102-e4c71b9d/ablation-001.json and

**What worked:** the new branch passed architecture gradient/shape tests,
all 14 unit tests, pinned simulator verification, and complete real matches,
without illegal actions, simulator truncations or time limit guards.

**What did not work:** no detected strength gain: original control ranks
[8,8] (mean 8.0, top4 0%) and pooled extractor ranks [8,8]
(mean 8.0, top4 0%). Candidate-control mean rank delta 0.
Two extra-layer models did not rescue insufficient learning. Control
end-turn fallback count 8, candidate 11.

**Possible reasons:** the short 128-step exposure and large observation/action
space are plausible causes of generally weak policy learning. It is not
possible to isolate architecture as the cause from this tiny sample.

**Recommendation:** do not replace main's extractor on this evidence.
First run larger matched-budget and >=10 held-out-seed controlled studies,
potentially testing action-loop controls, without colliding with concurrent
PRs. The model weights exist only in a 30-day Actions artifact, not permanent
storage. Neither a statistically significant difference nor TavernArena
performance is established.
