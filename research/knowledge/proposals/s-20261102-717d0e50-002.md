# s-20261102-717d0e50 R002 — Public quality-aware Freeze test: negative pilot

## Hypothesis and rationale
R001 indiscriminate Freeze led to mean rank 7.7 vs baseline 6.9 on 10 pairs. R002 conditions Freeze on same player's public shop minion tier and stats, while guarding against unfreezing. Compare baseline, blind Freeze, quality Freeze across fresh independent seeds.

## Evidence (real, not inferred)
- Verified true pinned TavernGym 1.1.0 engine (source hash 6d8712c93022ba11, observe2 4017/447, action mask 245).
- 30 actual complete eight-seat games: 10 per arm, seeds 29201–29210, all Game.is_over true, no invalid actions, no truncated combat, no simulator turn limits.
- [Successful isolated workflow](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41417682184), source SHA 5610b37eec2f0fa3b2926bdc5b49b232ce9c5dac.
- Mean ranks (lower better): baseline 6.8, blind 7.1, quality 7.2. Top4: 1/10 baseline, 0/10 both Freeze variants. First: zero for all. Freeze actions: 0, 44, 32.
- All raw `experiments/s-20261102-717d0e50/control2-*.json`, `blind2-*.json`, `002-*.json` and metrics `research/sessions/s-20261102-717d0e50/metrics-002.json` are in the session-owned branch.
- No PPO training (0 steps), no model checkpoint.

## Bugs/lessons
Initial action confused old V1 OBS_FLOATS 3227 and V2 OBS2_FLOATS 4017 and would have incorrectly decoded shop from V2 float offsets. The independent fix reads the player's public V1 observation only for documented legacy shop offsets and verifies V2 contract separately. A synthetic fixture then caused test failure; both failures and fixes are recorded with real workflow links in failures/002-dual-observation-layout.md.

## Recommendation
Do not merge quality-filtered freezing into common baseline. Ten matched seeds do not prove general harm but show **no competitive gain**; fewer Freeze actions alone is not a useful objective. Investigate more influential early-game economy, combat strength and buy selection with fresh held-out tests; avoid repeat of other agents' pending hypotheses.
