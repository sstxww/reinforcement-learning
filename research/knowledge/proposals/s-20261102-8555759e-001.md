## Reusable lesson

**Do not replace default choice action priority with unconditional highest-index legal priority.** It showed a negative screening result against seven official scripted bots. This does not prove the default tie-break is globally optimal or that positional choice preferences cause the loss; six seeds are too few for that conclusion.

A better future design would inspect action semantics and observed card/hero/board context to rank actual option values. Add distinct held-out seeds and preserve deterministic paired testing. Related yet still unmerged PPO evidence in PR #9 / #15 also shows additional 256→1280 PPO steps did not improve small-sample rank, reinforcing the need for behavioral rather than purely budget-driven changes.
