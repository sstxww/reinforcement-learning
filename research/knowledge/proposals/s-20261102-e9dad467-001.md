## Findings, not overclaims
Positive engineering result: engine supports nonzero-seat policy actions and independently records correct controlled-seat placement. Distinct rankings in these tiny samples motivate seat-aware metrics but **do not demonstrate intrinsic seat bias, better strategy, or a generalizable effect**. Different seats can receive different heroes and change RNG consumption. This is not PPO training: actual training steps 0, model none.

## Failure avoidance
- Do not hard-code placements[0] when varying controlled seat.
- Guard illegal actions, wrong observe2 dimensions, stalled/out-of-budget games and simulator truncation.
- Attach the actual full-match seed, run link, policy hash and controlled seat to each receipt.
- Avoid shared train.yml and shared model artifacts; use session-unique workflow and ownership paths.
- Do not promote any Best model from this baseline-only, very small study.

## Next test
Use held-out seeds and other seats; eventually evaluate identical PPO checkpoints under seat rotation with adequate samples and paired uncertainty, keeping the engine fixed. Do not confuse descriptive seat sensitivity with causal seat advantage.
