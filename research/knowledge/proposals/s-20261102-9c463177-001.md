## Proposal
Do **not** replace V0 with rightmost choice based on this pilot. The
unimpressive absolute ranks and n=4 do not establish quality; increase
paired independent seeds (e.g., 30) and characterize which choice types
and card IDs are chosen before evaluating action-specific heuristics.
Avoid mechanically choosing rightmost as if it were a learned advantage.
All logs/receipts must be independently checked before integration.
