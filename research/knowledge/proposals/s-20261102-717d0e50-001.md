# s-20261102-717d0e50 R001 — Freeze timing negative screening proposal

## Hypothesis
A single masked-legal Tavern Freeze (action 2) on low-resource turns 4..9 before EndTurn might improve performance relative to the default never-Freeze baseline. All code is session-owned; no shared baseline modification.

## Genuine evidence

- TavernGym 1.1.0 source fingerprint 6d8712c93022ba11, observe2 4017/447/245.
- 10 matched seeds (29101-29110), **20 completed actual eight-seat games**, Game.is_over() true; zero illegal actions, simulator turn limits or combat truncations.
- Candidate average rank **7.7**, baseline **6.9** (smaller better), candidate-control +0.8. Top-four candidate 0/10 versus baseline 1/10; first place candidate 0/10 versus 1/10.
- Candidate made 35 Freeze actions, all legal; at most one per turn; 477 candidate controlled decisions, 503 baseline decisions.
- Full raw receipts at experiments/s-20261102-717d0e50/001-29101.json through 001-29110.json and control-29101.json through control-29110.json. Metrics at research/sessions/s-20261102-717d0e50/metrics-001.json.
- Pure strategy: 0 PPO training steps, no model checkpoint. Short-lived artifact 13128190086 expires 2026-12-02.

## Reusable decision
**Do not promote unconditional end-of-turn low-gold Freeze.** Pilot outcome descriptively favors the original baseline; 10 seeds and one strong control outlier are insufficient for a reliable general strength conclusion. For a follow-up, condition Freeze on publicly visible shop quality and test on fresh seeds. Retain negative data rather than claim a gain.
