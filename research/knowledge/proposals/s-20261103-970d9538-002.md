# V5 P1 proposal: exact restore lineage, without hiding unverified legacy state

Integrate the specific hash and cumulative-step validation from existing [PR #124](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/pull/124), preserving its author credit and regression semantics. New output checkpoint must carry exact SHA256, and resumed PPO's internal `num_timesteps` must match the metadata. Legacy schema-1 checkpoints without SHA may remain loadable but **not integrity-verified**.

This is deliberately different from the stronger ZIP/optimizer preflight already integrated by [PR #116](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/pull/116); a Release-byte verifier protects storage provenance, while in-trainer step/hash checks protect the training transition. A self-declared digest is not independent proof of trustworthy source. Keep explicit source tag, engine, hash, optimizer verification and separate save output.

Cannot claim completion of centralized continuation until production `.github/workflows/train.yml` eliminates silent random initialization on missing Artifacts and supports verified durable Release input and reliable long-term checkpoint output. Hold model promotion: observed raw PPO fails to purchase on many legal-BUY states and ranks eighth; bounded continuation is an infrastructure smoke, not a competitive gain.
