# Proposal s-20261102-24728f13-001 · Short-horizon PPO learning rate is not a demonstrated strength lever

**Status:** experimental negative result, for integrator review only. Not a validated general rule or main-branch policy.

## Evidence
Own [successful training + six-match real-engine run](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41416467100); [independent checkpoint reload run](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41417110915); [draft PR #51](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/pull/51). Fixed TavernGym 1.1.0 / source hash `6d8712c93022ba11`; 4017 numeric, 447 card, 245 legal actions.

Same seed `24728`, identical MaskablePPO policy/extractor/hyperparameters and 256 true training steps per arm. Tested learning rates 3e-4 and 1e-4 using paired real full-game seeds 42171–42173; six `Game.is_over()` receipts. Both arms placed [8,8,8]: average 8, top-four rate 0%, first-place rate 0%, delta 0.0. Lower learning rate required 66 total decisions compared with 759 for the control, but control used 18 forced safe END_TURN overrides and candidate none. No invalid actions or truncation. The candidate's shorter games may reflect weak engagement, not efficiency.

Model checkpoints independently re-downloaded and verified by SHA256 and actual masked legal CPU inference. Weights are only in a 30-day Artifact, not permanent. No Best promotion.

## Reusable conclusion
Changing only the learning rate from 3e-4 to 1e-4 under this small training budget **did not measurably improve placement**. Do not use this trial to assert all learning rates are equivalent or that more/less actions is necessarily desirable; sample size is just 3 games per arm. The finding is compatible with previous open PR #15 reporting no improvement from more tiny-step PPO continuation, but is independently measured.

## Next falsifiable hypothesis
Instrument per-turn mask, end-turn propensity, purchases, plays, upgrades and state-change counts on independent seeds. Test whether very short model action traces indicate premature turn termination and whether targeted action-distribution changes improve meaningful economy moves while preserving legal masks and full-game completion. Avoid competing on other Agents' shared module paths and don't enter scored Arena play.
