# V5 P1 integration: isolate the Release write token

Current main after PR #117 restored a registered and checksum-verified Release, but the training job was granted repository contents:write while it installs Python packages, bootstraps a third-party engine, deserializes a PPO model and performs rollout inference.

This patch restricts the dependency-executing train job to contents:read. It retains the manual-only fail-closed release restore and raw-model paired evaluation, stages candidate weights with source/candidate SHA256 and monotone step lineage, verifies its checkpoint archive and raw model receipt, and uploads an exact-run artifact. A separate publisher job is the *only* contents:write job, runs after the training gate, rechecks the source artifact hashes/evaluation fields, and publishes an explicitly unpromoted Release with download-byte comparison.

Archive preflight is not optimizer resumption evidence by itself; source code tests do not prove Release authorization. The full production workflow still needs an independently observed bounded manual PPO continuation and persisted download validation. No model is promoted on this patch.

Behavioral limitation: the real 1408-step V5 PPO game in #154, Action 41450329264, seed99231, had 38 BUY-legal decision exposures but 0 BUY, 70 FREEZE and 42 REFRESH, rank8 with no fallback. #107 suggests force BUY alone is insufficient on three explored seeds and identifies Play/Cancel cycles. Do not expand bulk training until progressive-action behavior is tested against independent games.

Safe gates: own authentic eight-seat smoke, CI exact HEAD, new-main compatibility, least privilege, no secrets, no Arena ranked play.
