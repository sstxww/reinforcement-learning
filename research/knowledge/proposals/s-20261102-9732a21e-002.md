- Aggregate: candidate **125 decision points where some buy action was mask-legal; 0 model buys**. These are repeated actions in at most two seeded episodes, not 125 independent economic opportunities. No causality or generalization claim from two seeds.
- This is an action-family selection pathology more specific than simply “not enough PPO steps.” The 30,000-step Play/Cancel loop can change the public observation every step without genuine strategic progress: `same_public_observation_transitions=0` in that trace, so state-hash *equality alone* is not a robust no-progress detector.

## Reproducible next tests and cautions
1. Record own-seat legal purchase mask, affordability/gold (public feature 7), action type and per-turn repetition; include raw model selection and fallback/override counters separately.
2. Examine reward/advantage distribution and how Cancel/Play cycles are weighted during PPO rollouts. Code-level cause is **not proven** by the observed episode trace; changing LR alone is unsupported.
3. Compare pure PPO, fixed model with an explicitly labelled *hybrid* legal-buy override/turn-budget intervention, and same-seed rules baseline, with game-completion and rank denominators. Our isolated P3 [run 41429774484](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41429774484) performs this bounded study; at publication of this proposal the outcome was **not yet verified**.
4. Treat a failed full-game safety limit as a failed evaluation, **never a placement of 8**. Stop automatic promotion and large training until real heldout pure-model completion is reliable.
5. Archive rejected model weights with exact SHA256 and Release re-download; do not overwrite good checkpoints. This release survives ephemeral Artifact expiration but proves durability, **not** performance.
