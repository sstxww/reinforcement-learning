**Conclusion / learning:** opening hero selection may influence individual trajectories, but excluding it did not rescue the last-slot heuristic on held-out games. The `Choose` action band includes hero, Discover, spell targets and trinket decisions, so generic positional biases are not semantics-aware. Do not merge either candidate into shared Best. Suggest learning/estimating option value *conditional on choice category*.
