### Tested single hypothesis
A rule baseline that ignores hero powers might gain from using one mask-legal hero power before an otherwise idle EndTurn. Only action 4 or 5 is allowed, and only once per turn, never after the 60-action safety threshold. Implemented in an isolated new module, without altering the common baseline.

### Reusable lesson
Do not take a favorable four-seed screening result as proof. Always freeze the candidate and validate it against separate seeds. A hero-agnostic once-per-turn idle activation is not supported on our eight fresh holdout seeds. A future hypothesis should incorporate power identity, cost, and useful timing rather than apply a global rule without evidence.
