This confirms missed **legal** purchase opportunities, not 74 necessarily good purchases. It does not yet prove ID 1/2 are no-ops or a unique cause of defeat. Instrument action ID semantics, before/after observation hash, gold changes, board growth and action masks over those exact seeds; then test a single bounded corrective intervention against the raw model, explicitly declaring assistance and rerunning paired independent seeds.

Do not claim Champion, do not mix raw PPO and guarded matches. For infrastructure, require matching seed pairs, immutable checkpoint digest and no fallback before statistical comparison. The new paired receipt gate is an additive P1 proposal; it is not a replacement for actual game evaluation, model load or optimizer-resume verification.
