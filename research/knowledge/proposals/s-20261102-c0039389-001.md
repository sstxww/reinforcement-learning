## Problem
On current `main@315b068bac5ab6d8403c3c9b18f8c101c0cded5d`, both `tavern_ai.canary.run` and `tavern_ai.evaluate` assume controlled seat zero (`placements[0]`). Seed-only robustness does not establish seat robustness.

## Proposed controlled experiment

Run one separate candidate-controlled canary at seat four. Record all eight true final ranks plus `controlled_seat` and rank consistency checks. Full real TavernGym end state, legal actions, no truncated combats or turn safety limit are hard gates. Experiments: `experiments/s-20261102-c0039389/`; report: `reports/s-20261102-c0039389/seat-comparison.json`; source: `tavern_ai/seat_position_experiment.py`.

## Related findings and avoidance of duplicated work

## Interpretation and limits
No gradient steps, no checkpoint; evaluate it as **engineering sensitivity** not a gameplay improvement. A paired seat effect is confounded by changing hero and bot assignments. Three pairs are exploratory and do not prove bias/strength. Do not mark Best from these findings.
