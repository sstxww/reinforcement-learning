## Engineering insight
When a policy controls a seat other than zero, `placements[0]` in the existing receipt validator CLI is **not that policy's rank**. Always pair `controlled_seat` with `controlled_placement=placements[controlled_seat]`. Use the actual Game's observe2/legal_mask/step APIs with the correct controlled seat; preserve all eight placements and a complete-game receipt.

## What does not work / limits
- Changing seat does **not** by itself demonstrate stronger strategy or seat-caused bias: hero allocation and other bot seats also change; n=2 is tiny.
- Model trained: none (0 PPO timesteps). Do not promote any checkpoint, assign an Elo, or infer improvements from this engineering proof.
- Avoid repeating four-game comparisons with no improvement hypothesis; use larger held-out seeds and seat-stratification when sample budget allows.
