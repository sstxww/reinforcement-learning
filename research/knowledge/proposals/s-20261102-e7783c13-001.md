# Proposed strict paired receipt evidence gate (not yet merged policy)

Reuse: canonical `tavern_ai.receipt_integrity.validate_integrity` from main. No duplicate simulator, trainer, checkpoint writer or registry.

Improvement: fail closed when baseline/model receipt sets differ by seed/seat, candidate hashes differ, simulator provenance mismatches, incomplete or malformed receipt occurs, or model records include explicit guard/fallback. Report paired placement, top-four and first-place stats. Always withhold automatic Champion promotion.

Motivation: eighth-place evidence has mixed raw PPO, end guards and explicit overrides across sessions. Such arms cannot be pooled as raw model strength.

Limits: an evidence-format validation tool, not a gameplay fix or new strength claim. Synthetic test fixtures never stand in for authentic matches. New training in this round: zero; dedicated GitHub Actions real-game proof pending.
