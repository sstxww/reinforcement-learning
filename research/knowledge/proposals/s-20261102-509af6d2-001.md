# Research proposal s-20261102-509af6d2-001: Opening Tavern upgrade delay, uncertain signal

**Status: provisional / not yet demonstrated as a robust gain.** Do not change the production baseline or promote a PPO `Best` based on this record.

## Question
Does deferring the deterministic baseline's opening turn-2 tavern-upgrade action to turn 3 trade early shop progression for stronger board/economy and improved eight-seat placements?

## Self-contained reproducible evidence
- Separate original baseline vs opt-in `DelayedOpeningUpgradePolicy`, single controlled seat 0 against seven official scripted opponents.
- Exact pinned TavernGym 1.1.0, observe2, source hash `6d8712c93022ba11`, card slots 447, numeric features 4017, masked discrete actions 245.
- Fixed candidate implementation `tavern_ai/s_20261102_509af6d2.py` (SHA256 `7620db9bb2925336114ff133fd99af7e5f5afbc38b599006be2fb9ffa88ba692`) and tests with mandatory-choice, play-first, action-budget and legal-mask invariants.
- [Initial run](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41415050033) success; seeds `38101–38104`.
- [Fresh held-out confirmation](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41415518707) success; seeds `38201–38216`; no candidate changes between both runs.
- All **40 full real TavernGym games** finished and the receipt validator passed. See `experiments/s-20261102-509af6d2/` and `reports/s-20261102-509af6d2/`.
- Across 20 independent paired seeds, baseline: mean rank **7.40**, first 0/20, top4 1/20. Candidate: mean rank **6.80**, first 2/20, top4 3/20.
- Mean paired rank difference **-0.60**, approximate 95% t interval **[-1.84, +0.64]**; interval includes no improvement or a small regression. Median paired difference is zero.
- Not a PPO training experiment; **0 gradient timesteps**, no candidate weights/checkpoint. Never conflate this candidate with PPO resumes or Arena Elo.

## Learning transferable to other sessions
1. Preserve **same-seed paired comparisons** with real final ranks; a single new strategy win can hide many last-place games.
2. Verify more unseen seeds without any code/parameter changes when the first four seeds look promising. Here initial gain `-0.75` shrank to `-0.60` over 20 pairs, and confidence is still too broad to claim a gain.
3. Treat the early Tavern upgrade as a hypothesis, not a settled best opening. No irreversible integration is supported by these data.
4. Preserve original and candidate candidate-controlled receipts separately, including `Game.is_over()`, all eight placements, decision counts, engine fingerprint, policy SHA and Git commit.
5. Use branch-isolated, bounded, paths-filtered Actions to avoid concurrency, repeated-trigger loops and model overwrites.

## Negative/limitations
- Candidate is volatile: ranks include both 1st and many 8th places. Most paired differences are zero or small; outlier finishes dominate the average.
- The baseline itself scores poorly against scripted bots; improving a weak baseline is not evidence of tournament-level strength.
- We did not test a learned PPO policy, a battle-arena scoring environment, a comprehensive strategy grid, or adaptive features.
- The 30-day Actions artifacts are only backups; immutable JSON data and reports remain in this branch, subject to repository retention. No model storage has been created.

## Follow-up for another review/round
If opening timing is worth continued study, verify a state-aware *conditional* defer decision using accurately decoded public observation features and a new disjoint held-out seed set. Pre-register the candidate, avoid tuning on those seeds, and require a stronger confidence interval and credible adversary diversity before promoting any `Best`. Any implementation must respect session isolation and the confirmed action-mask contract.
