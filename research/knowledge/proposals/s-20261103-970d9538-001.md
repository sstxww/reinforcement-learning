**Source credit**: PR #130 authored the focused ZIP/member-CRC and MaskablePPO algorithm checks and passed an authentic latest-head CI at commit `1786e792fcdfd5a1b5998803384d393c328b58ce`. Why it matters: SHA256 integrity alone does not imply that externally stored checkpoint bytes form a valid ZIP or contain readable members. These checks reject malformed archives and unsupported algorithms *before* expensive optional deserialization, but **do not** establish that optimizer state can actually resume. Do not advertise this as complete model recovery or durable output publication.

Main follow-up: choose one well-proven fail-closed Release resume + atomic durable-save implementation from overlapping current drafts; require exact-head test+original eight-seat game+true optimizer continuation and immutable checkpoint evidence before merge.
