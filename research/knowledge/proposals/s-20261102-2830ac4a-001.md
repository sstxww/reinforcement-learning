## What was / was not learned
The optional policy safely completes real matches using the pinned engine and valid masks. **No improvement is observed or supported** in this tiny sample. The exact match placements and decision counts under the control and candidate suggest the experimental conditions may never have caused a distinct decision, so this result alone cannot isolate the value of selling. Do not integrate as a superior default strategy.
