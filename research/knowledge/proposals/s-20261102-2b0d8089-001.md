# Research proposal: s-20261102-2b0d8089 R001

**Status: unmerged, short pilot; NOT validated as a shared strength improvement.**

## Finding

With TavernGym **1.1.0** (`6d8712c93022ba11`), `signed_log1p(r)` preserves reward sign, but a two-arm **256-step MaskablePPO** pilot did **not** show better last-update critic diagnostics or real-game placements. It should **not** be enabled by default.

- Fixed train seed `20261102`; test seeds `27001,27002`; 2 games each of baseline/raw PPO/log PPO.
- Mean ranks (lower better): baseline **6.5**, raw PPO with turn cap **7.5**, signed-log PPO with same turn cap **8.0**.
- Critic last `value_loss`: **0.063789** raw, **0.071282** log. Explained variance: **0.08761** raw, **-0.52646** log.
- All six games were completed by TavernGym and validate without illegal action or game guard truncation. Evidence: `research/sessions/s-20261102-2b0d8089/001-summary.json` and six independent real-game JSON receipts.
- Successful run: https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41414058042.

## Failure mode worth sharing

An unconstrained, freshly trained mask-aware PPO model can continuously repeat **legal but non-progressing actions**. Run 41412952801 had actual training (512 steps), but model rollout stopped at the 30,000-iteration game-driver safety limit. Legal action mask compliance alone does **not** guarantee a complete game.

In the isolated experiment, after 25 own-seat actions on a turn, a legal END TURN is selected; model canaries then finish. The policy is explicitly marked `model_bounded_25`. Safeguard use was 6–8 forced end-turn actions per model game. This is an engineering observation, **not** evidence of higher playing strength.

## Interpretation

A transformed reward can trivially change the scale of value loss, even if policy quality worsens. Only the last update was captured; there is no measured variance trajectory and sample size is **n=2**. No significance claim, no model improvement claim, no Arena ranking claim.
