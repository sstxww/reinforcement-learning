## What to reuse

Use fail-closed legal action masking and **explicitly label guarded PPO** when evaluating undertrained policies. A short per-turn budget can enable real game-completion evidence, but it can alter policy behavior and confound an apples-to-apples strength test. Other unmerged PRs #12/#17/#18 independently used caps 48/45/65; do not assert a universal optimal cutoff from our 32-action two-seed experiment.
