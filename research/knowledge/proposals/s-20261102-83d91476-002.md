# R002 proposal: isolate Refresh/Freeze over-selection with a buying intervention

Authentic R001 evidence: V2 PPO had 74 legal BUY exposures but purchased zero times across two new heldout games; 222/248 actions were Refresh(1) or Freeze(2) by pinned upstream action ABI. Model ranked [8,8], baseline [6,7].

R002 differs from other rule-strategy experiments: frozen PPO action is computed **first**, then only proposed Refresh/Freeze with legally available BUY can be replaced by first legal BUY. Report all interventions and classify as an external override, not PPO improvement. New seeds 45113–45116 are triple paired (raw model, override, baseline). No new training or model architecture changes.

Small-sample data are exploratory and should decide whether to instrument raw logits/economy more deeply, NOT automatically promote weights or claim a conclusive strategy gain. PR depends on R001 #90; do not independently merge while #90 is outstanding.
