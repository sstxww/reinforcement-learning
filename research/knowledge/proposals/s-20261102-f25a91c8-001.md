# P2 proposal — do not mix guarded and raw model rankings

Source: `research/sessions/s-20261102-f25a91c8/round-001.md`.

Main already contains genuine rank-8 evidence: [Actions 41428098018](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41428098018) documented 74 legal-BUY decision exposures and zero buys for a V2 PPO candidate across two games. Also observed 222/248 actions were IDs 1/2, not necessarily identical states. Another real 1,408-step candidate in `reports/s-20261102-68099929/003-buy-opportunity.json` selected zero buys across 35 eligible decision exposures on seed 99128. These are observational associations, NOT proof that any one action is the unique reason for eighth place.

The existing candidate receipt schema includes `policy: model_guarded` with `guarded: false`; even without triggered forced END_TURN, this is a guarded-policy configuration. Treat raw unassisted evidence and assisted evidence separately. Before asserting improvement, require complete real eight-seat receipts, exact pinned TavernGym contract, matched seed+seat pairs, same candidate SHA, zero guard/fallback, and uncertainty intervals. This session proposes `tavern_ai/paired_evidence_gate_f25a91c8.py` as an additive offline gate, not as a replacement for source model evaluation or a Champion decision.

No claims of new training, new candidate games, guaranteed strategy gain or formal Arena performance are made.
