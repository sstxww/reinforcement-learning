## Distinct hypothesis and proposed test

The model may fail to convert legal, often selected actions into *productive and advancing* game state. Merely counting action IDs or legal mask exposures cannot distinguish ineffective repetitions, hidden game consequences or planned multi-step sequences.

This session adds a self-contained **public-observation transition probe** without changing existing policy. For every selected action, compare pinned `observe2` pre/post within the same turn; count changed feature/card slots, show raw action family and BUY mask-legal exposures, and label forced ends. Use one same-seed genuine TavernGym baseline/model pair as a diagnostic smoke, not a win-rate comparison. A matching projection is **not** proof of zero internal state change, and no private information enters the policy.
