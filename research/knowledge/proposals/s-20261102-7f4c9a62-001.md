## Hypothesis
With the same train seed 70141 and MaskablePPO architecture, ent_coef=0.02 might improve short-term exploration relative to ent_coef=0.0, yielding lower seat-0 mean ranking against seven scripted bots.

## Measurements and counterexample
Two independent PPO models were actually trained for 128 steps each (256 total), then each played 2 genuine full eight-seat games on common seeds 31041 and 31042:
- Unmodified baseline ranks [8,6], mean 7.0, top4 0/2, wins 0/2.
- Entropy 0.0 ranks [8,8], mean 8.0, top4 0/2, wins 0/2.
- Entropy 0.02 ranks [8,8], mean 8.0, top4 0/2, wins 0/2.
- Recorded real model-match receipt: experiments/s-20261102-7f4c9a62/001-31041.json, 153 decisions, full placements [8,5,6,4,1,7,3,2], completed true, no simulator protective truncation.
- All selected actions were masked for legality and all complete game receipts validated.
- Reproducible details, model SHA256 and metrics: reports/s-20261102-7f4c9a62/entropy-comparison.json.

## Interpretation and reuse
There is NO empirical superiority of entropy 0.02 in this tiny test. Both 128-step PPO models performed poorly on the two held-out seeds. We cannot conclude either coefficient is globally better/worse or that shared ranks imply identical action trajectories.
Useful validated engineering observation: isolated Actions training, deterministic version checks, model hashes, and real complete games can run without touching the shared train workflow. The Artifact expires after 30 days; weights are NOT permanently preserved.

Recommend: do NOT merge either early model as a champion checkpoint. Repeat with >=2048 steps/arm, >=20 matched evaluation seeds, additional train seeds, confidence intervals and legality/CPU-time metrics before any strength claim.
