# R002 proposal: matched raw-policy evaluation before expanding PPO

R001 reliably restored a published 1408-step V5 model, trained exactly 128 additional steps and persisted a 1536-step checkpoint; engineering success did not imply strength improvement. Its raw candidate seed 90312 ranked eighth. One unmatched baseline game is not a paired comparison.

R002 will run new fixed same-seat paired heldout seed triples (90321/90322) for baseline, old raw model and resumed raw model, no guard. Record completed-game status, masks, opportunity-conditioned purchases, action-family loops and outcome rank per same seed. Distinguish **raw model failure to finish** from an eighth-place finished game, and guard-affected performance from raw PPO.

The evidence will help decide whether to test an action/decision policy correction instead of merely increasing PPO steps; no Champion until independently paired positive rank evidence. Not an auto-promotable hypothesis.
