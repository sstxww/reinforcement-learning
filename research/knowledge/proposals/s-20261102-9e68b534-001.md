**Hypothesis tested at engineering level, not a validated performance gain:** A model should not become Best solely because its observed mean rank or top-four fraction improves over a few matches. Compute seeded game-level bootstrap confidence intervals of average placement, Wilson 95% confidence intervals of first-place and top-four rates, and paired rank differences matched on seeds. Reject duplicate seeds, incomplete receipts, nonpermutation eight-seat placements and unequal paired seed sets.

**Caveats:** n=4 is not a valid strength claim; nonparametric bootstrap is coarse for tiny n. A Wilson interval can remain broad despite a 0% or 100% observed success rate. Fixed paired seeds help but opponent randomness, policy deterministic choice and game dependencies still matter. At n>=30 the flag can be lifted for screening but no statistical improvement is guaranteed. This session does **not** train PPO, claim Best or play Arena ranked.
