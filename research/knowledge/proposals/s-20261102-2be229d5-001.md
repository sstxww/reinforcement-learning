# Proposal: make the canonical evaluator reject assisted/incompatible model games

Status: PROPOSAL, not established shared knowledge. Session `s-20261102-2be229d5`, R001.

Main's `tavern_ai.evaluate` originally reported mean rank/top4 on returned records without independently screening for incomplete/truncated/fallback-assisted games, model digest drift, misaligned paired seeds or changed engine contracts. The isolated implementation adds fail-closed receipt checks and conservative paired uncertainty while preserving historical CLI/API.

It is **not** a claim of policy or training improvement. The raw model may still stall and fail, which must be reported as failure rather than replaced silently by a guarded game. A paired n=1–3 mean rank cannot establish strength; independent seeds and seat rotations remain separate future requirements.

Existing main has several independent candidate Release verifiers/paired-evidence PRs. Integrate this change only once, after checking overlap and final-head CI, and do not create a competing model registry or Champion.
