# Proposal — Hero-power reserve rule (session s-20261102-3a93dae9, round 001)

**Status: NOT VERIFIED; do not modify common baseline or promote candidate model.**

Question: does using a legal hero power once per turn from turn 3, costing at most 3 gold and preserving 3 gold, outperform the current V0 baseline?

Evidence: exact TavernGym 1.1.0 source hash 6d8712c93022ba11. Separate six-seed paired real-match runs [41416155846](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41416155846) and [41416624735](https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41416624735), all complete. Candidate vs baseline average placements 6.00 vs 7.67 in first batch, **7.83 vs 7.00** in independent second batch. Overall 12 paired games, 6.92 vs 7.33, one first place and one top-four candidate; baseline zero wins but one top-four. This small result does not prove improvement. Seven games actually used a hero power; five had no usable ability. Not robust across independent seeds.

Historical caveat: initial zero-cost pilot had zero actions; second batch receipt policy label was stale, although SHA256 and versioned source precisely identify actual actions. The third independent run corrected metadata. See research/sessions/s-20261102-3a93dae9/round-001.md and failures/.

Reusable learning:
- Record actual intervention counts, not just paired game rank.
- Preserve original provenance when fixing metadata; never rewrite old results to make them look clean.
- Distinguish active and passive hero populations in future hero-power experiments.
- Use held-out independent seeds and report contradictory evidence, not only favorable samples.

Next proposal: stratified evaluation per hero-power family with matched seeds; require sufficient active-skill games, a fixed opponent pool, and uncertainty estimates. Integrator review before production changes. No TavernArena ranking.
