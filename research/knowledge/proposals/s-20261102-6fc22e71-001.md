**Status:** integration proposal; source-input validation tests are synthetic. No new trained model, win-rate gain or Champion.

**Observed:** Main's latest 1,408-step candidate has three registered paired heldout ranks [8,8,8]. PR #103's pinned-engine guarded V2 evaluation saw 0 buys in 521 legal-BUY decision observations, with safety-forced END actions; stacked PR #107's explicit purchase override caused 46 buys but no rank improvement and further guard use. Therefore the important problem is not simply the total training-step count or zero purchases.

**Potential reporting error:** model-controlled receipts with a legal safety override can be accidentally treated as raw PPO model competition results, especially when the receipt does not explicitly record guard counts. A completed match does not prove that the model can progress by itself.

**One additive fix:** a conservative, paired, source-independent evidence gate under `tavern_ai/paired_evidence_s_6fc22e71.py`. It validates real complete eight-seat receipt *shapes* and engine; matches controlled seat and seed, refuses mixed checkpoint hashes; produces per-game—not per-action—bootstrap rank intervals and only labels raw-policy evidence if the receipt explicitly reports no safety-guard interventions. Missing guard reporting means **unknown**. This is a quality gate, not a strategy change or replacement for genuine model inference.

**Falsifiable follow-up:** On independent seeds, instrument raw model choice-state cycles (Play↔Cancel) and the public observation before/after each legal action. Compare raw V2, guarded V2 and deterministic baseline under identical engine, seat, seed and strict termination rules. Measure buy-legal states, buy selections, unspent gold (if derived from own public observations), board/hand progression, end-turn/guard counts, and completion separately. Only if a narrow behavior patch changes those signals should bounded additional PPO continuation be attempted.
