## Test and evidence
- Compare original numeric features vs signed-log `sign(x) * log1p(abs(clamp(x,-500,500)))` with all other PPO architecture/settings held equal.
- True CPU MaskablePPO training: 256 steps each, seed 71002; paired eval seeds 32001/32002/32003, three complete 8-player simulator matches for each arm.
- Both arms used deterministic model inference and an identical *legal* per-turn END_TURN cap of 45.
- Raw placement [8,8,8], mean 8.0, top-4 0%. Signed-log placement [8,8,8], mean 8.0, top-4 0%. Paired delta [0,0,0]. **No observed improvement; statistically inconclusive for longer training**.
- All six games ended via real `Game.is_over()`, no illegal-action assertion failure, truncated combat 0 and no simulator turn-limit end. These are real engine receipts, not manual simulations.

## Effective vs ineffective changes
- **Reliability finding in this limited test:** first unbounded raw-policy game ran into `Full-game safety limit exceeded` (run 41412975503). After adding the same legal 45-action per-turn guard to both model executors, the rerun finished all six matches (run 41413617114). This does not prove a competitive advantage, and the guard changes the policy's action distribution.
- **Not observed to improve performance:** signed-log scaling at only 256 steps per model, 3 paired seeds. Do not silently change mainline extractor based on this experiment.
- **Unresolved:** small sample, extremely short training, no V0-baseline strength comparison, no action-distribution histogram, no train-seed replication or confidence interval. Both models placed last in all held-out games.
