# Research proposal s-20261102-6d2f9a71-001 — MaskablePPO action-budget safeguard and entropy ablation

**Status: experimental candidate; no validated competitive improvement.**

## Claim 1 — model free-action loops can block full games

On pinned TavernGym 1.1.0 (`source_hash=6d8712c93022ba11`, float features 4017, card slots 447, action mask 245), a freshly trained 256-step MaskablePPO policy selected legal no-cost actions repeatedly. The raw full-game runner hit the 30,000-iteration guard before `Game.is_over()`. This **failed** run is independently inspectable: https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41413283468 . No valid game receipt was recorded from the failure.

A separate, mask-respecting 40-action-per-turn guard in `tavern_ai/guarded_match.py` yielded **4 complete eight-seat games** on seeds 61001 and 61002 across 2 independently trained models, with 0 illegal actions, 0 simulator combat truncations and 0 turn-limit ends. Run: https://github.com/sstxww/reinforcement-learning/actions/runs/41413940695 . The per-turn guard fired 1 and 2 times per seed, respectively, for each arm. The fix is an engineering workaround and should be compared across other policies rather than assumed universally optimal.

## Claim 2 — changing ent_coef 0.01 → 0.04 did not improve tiny-sample placement

Training seed 77331; fresh weights, same TavernExtractor architecture and optimizer, both arms trained for **256 real PPO steps** (512 preserved steps total), with a distinct entropy coefficient (0.01 reference, 0.04 candidate). Independent paired seeds 61001, 61002; each model controlled one seat against seven official scripted bots.

| Metric | Entropy .01 | Entropy .04 |

**Observed mean rank change: 0.0**. This does *not* establish parameter equality, nor does it establish stronger performance; both models are extremely undertrained and the sample size is two per arm. Real receipts: `experiments/s-20261102-6d2f9a71/001-61001.json`, `candidate-61002.json`, `reference-61001.json`, `reference-61002.json`. Full configuration and data: `research/sessions/s-20261102-6d2f9a71/round-001.md`, `research/sessions/s-20261102-6d2f9a71/metrics-001.json`.

## Recommendation for subsequent agents / integration owner

Do not port this entropy change into shared `train.py` based on these results. Consider upstreaming a well-tested masked end-turn budget to model inference only after confirming game-specific mandatory choices and not harming baseline performance. For entropy evaluation, train at least 8k–32k steps per arm if resources permit, pair 8–20+ independent seeds and inspect legal action diversity, mean decisions, top-four and rank confidence intervals. Record real elapsed execution and max train resource use in follow-up.
