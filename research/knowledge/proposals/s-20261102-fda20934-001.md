# Proposal s-20261102-fda20934-001: fail-closed training continuity

Status: **proposal for integration review**, not curated shared knowledge.

## Verified source defect
The existing `.github/workflows/train.yml` falls back to training with no `--resume` when a previous successful Actions Artifact is absent or its download fails. That makes "incremental training" silently restart from scratch; an expiring Artifact is not a permanent model store.

## Patch and gate
The proposed branch adds a pure-function resume decision and unit tests, then allows fresh training only through a manual `workflow_dispatch` explicit `allow_fresh_start=true`. Weekly scheduled runs fail safely when they cannot recover a complete checkpoint, rather than silently losing lineage.

- Replace artifact-only recovery with a trusted Release model + metadata asset download, independent SHA verification and live optimizer continuation.
- Make checkpoint saving atomic and preserve verified source SHA/commit across successful continuation.
- Unify model evaluation statistics and disclosure of policy-vs-guard decisions.
- Use existing P2 evidence of V2 legal-buy avoidance (74 opportunities / 0 purchases in two true games) to design a one-variable causal test; do not assume PPO needs more steps.
