PR #87 already integrated `models/registry.json`, which catalogs four candidate Releases and holds `best=null`, `champion=null`. Do **not** introduce a competing registry. The main field `latest` denotes an experimental unpromoted candidate (currently V2 1280 steps), **not** a verified durable restart. P1 is to verify **both** model.zip and model.metadata.json SHA256s, ZIP CRCs, engine metadata and step count before any load. PR #93 contributes an optional offline read-only supplement to the existing registry validator.

These checks are weaker than actual `MaskablePPO.load`, optimizer state continuation, model-controlled full eight-seat games, and heldout paired comparison. Do not mark V2 restart or Champion as proven from archive presence or manifest checks alone. Existing V1 256→384-step recovery was reported by a separate real Actions run; that result does not establish V2 fresh restore.

Verify with real games and small paired heldout tests before resuming training at scale.
