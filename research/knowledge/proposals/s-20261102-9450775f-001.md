# Proposal — V5 model archival and no-fallback rank-eight gate

**Claim level:** instrument and verification design, not a proven model improvement.

The current `train.py` resume checks engine metadata but does not verify published weight SHA256 or archive CRC before load. The scheduled workflow restores a 90-day Artifact and does not establish a durable Champion. Releases do provide SHA256 asset records, but those alone do not prove correct runtime load or future training.

An isolated checker now requires exact SHA256, ZIP CRC, SB3 optimizer archive member, matching pinned engine metadata and nonzero tracked total steps. A bounded real-game runner separates BUY availability, actual BUY choices and END with BUY legal, records repeated same-observation actions, and forbids model fallback. Incomplete games are diagnostic failures, not valid eight-seat receipts.

**Next integration decision:** keep as an opt-in review tool until full live Actions tests succeed; only then consider integrating model registry/CI and a real resumed-optimizer smoke test. Do not claim Champion promotion or a rank improvement from engineering tests.
