# P3 bounded hypothesis — replay-only Play→Cancel blacklist

**Evidence precondition:** V5 run #41428534974 on `model_sha256=503c5274...`, seed 927104, genuine pinned TavernGym, produced 301 model decisions, 300 in turn 1, zero END_TURN, zero buys despite 7 decisions with native legal buy actions, and the action trace repeatedly alternates Play index 46 / Cancel index 7. According to pinned engine source-audited index table in PR #75, action 7 is **Cancel**, not a generic other action. This session trace is `reports/s-20261102-902968f5/002-public-action-trace.json`; `completed=false`, `per_turn_action_budget_exceeded`. No full-game result is fabricated.

**Hypothesis:** when a model-chosen Play action is immediately followed by Cancel, blacklisting that exact Play ID for the remainder of the same turn will prevent recursive Play→Cancel and may allow legal other moves. The blacklisting is implemented using only prior public actions and the native legal mask. It leaves a sole legal move intact. This is a **changed model policy at inference time**, not additional PPO training and not a raw model performance result.

**Tests:** pure unit tests enforce that the guard only removes previously cancelled Play actions when alternatives exist, never invents legal moves, and never suppresses the sole legal Cancel. Two authentic real TavernGym eight-seat bounded games on exact old failure seed 927104 and independent seed 927106 are triggered in own finite workflow. Output is a guarded-policy report under `reports/`, **not** `experiments/..receipt.json` and cannot be interpreted as pure-model 8-player receipt. Incomplete game remains incomplete, rank reported only if actually `Game.is_over()` is true. Any positive result requires more seeds/seat rotation and independent evaluation before considering a real policy fix or increasing PPO steps.

**Caveat:** Before observing pending-target masks we cannot rule out that Cancel was the sole legal action immediately after Play, in which case masking Cancel would be wrong. The intervention therefore does **not** force a target choice or mask Cancel; it temporarily suppresses the failed Play on the next shop choice only.
